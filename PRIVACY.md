# Privacy Policy for still.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

still. does not collect, transmit or share any personal data or browsing information.

- The extension makes no network requests. Its contemplations are bundled with it.
- Your favourites, your pinned contemplation and your journal entries are stored only in your browser, on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and the developer cannot see them.
- There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or third-party services.
- The extension requests no browser permissions.

Removing the extension deletes its stored data from your browser.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bgallagher/contemplation-tab/issues.
